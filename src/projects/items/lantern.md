## What it is

Lantern strips an article down to its text and sets it in a calm column. You choose the typeface, the size and the line length once, and every article after that looks the way you like to read.

## The interesting part

Finding the article inside a page is the hard part. There is no reliable marker for "the bit people came to read", so Lantern scores each block by how much text it holds and how few links, and takes the best run of blocks.

It also remembers settings per device rather than per site. People want one reading setup, not forty.

## What I would change

I added themes too early. Three weeks went into colour schemes while the article finder still failed on a fifth of the pages I tried. The boring part should have come first.
